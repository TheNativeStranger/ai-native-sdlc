# Brand and UX

Starter rules. Replace them with your own; the policy owner seat signs off every change to this file.

1. Use the project's existing components before adding a new one.
2. Every screen works with a keyboard and has visible focus.
3. Error messages say what went wrong and what to do next.
4. Write interface text in the product's voice; no internal names.

When a rule cannot be met, or two rules contradict each other, say so under Flagged concerns. Do not pick a side quietly.
