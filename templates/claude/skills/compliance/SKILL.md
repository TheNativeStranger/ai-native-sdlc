# Compliance

Starter rules. Replace them with your own; the policy owner seat signs off every change to this file.

1. Collect no personal data the intent did not ask for.
2. Every state-changing action records who did what to which record and when.
3. Data that leaves the system is listed in the spec with its destination.
4. A change to how long data is kept is a flagged change.

When a rule cannot be met, or two rules contradict each other, say so under Flagged concerns. Do not pick a side quietly.
